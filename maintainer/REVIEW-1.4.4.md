## Scope

Native previews are temporary, sandboxed and separate from the application, with no Node.js/application bridge, restricted permissions/navigation/downloads and a bounded public-HTTPS resource broker. Code, math, copying, existing SVG rendering and ordinary image controls retain their distinct behavior.
